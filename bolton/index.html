Bolton context

Bolton has deep community roots in ESOL. EmployaLingua® was partly shaped here.

BRASS — Befriending Refugees and Asylum Seekers — is based in Bolton and was a founding design partner for EmployaLingua®. The platform's dignity-first approach was shaped in part by Bolton's refugee and asylum seeker communities.

Royal Bolton Hospital is one of the largest NHS employers in the North West. Bolton College serves thousands of ESOL learners annually. Bolton's manufacturing and logistics heritage creates strong Construction and Digital sector employment pathways.
