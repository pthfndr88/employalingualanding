Built for daily progress

Five minutes a day
builds a career.

EmployaLingua® is designed around one principle: small daily actions compound into employment outcomes. This is not a course with a start and end date. It is a daily habit that builds toward a job offer.

Daily missions — short, focused, sector-specific. Delivered as a push notification so learners never have to decide what to do next.

XP and streaks — progress learners can see and feel. Every mission completed, every streak maintained, moves them visibly closer to employment.

Instant bilingual feedback — the AI responds in English and their native language. Corrections feel like coaching, not assessment.

Job-readiness score — a live indicator your team can use to identify which learners are ready for employer referral before the programme ends.
