The Trapezium Model

Most platforms teach skills.
EmployaLingua® starts with identity.

Skills built on sand don't hold. A learner who doesn't understand who they are — their background, capability, and professional story — cannot build meaningful competence on top of it.

EmployaLingua®'s Trapezium Model structures every learner's journey through three axes — Know. Be. Do. — and four ascending pillars: Identity, Character, Competence, and Impact. The sequence is deliberate and irreversible. You cannot skip Identity and arrive at Impact.

KNOW · Identity

Who are you? Every learner receives a personalised identity statement before their path begins — written in English and their native language.

BE · Character

Values, working style, professional behaviours. Character determines how competence is applied in a real workplace — and how a learner shows up on day one.

DO · Competence

The sector-specific language, vocabulary, and professional register of the role — built on a stable foundation of identity and character.

DO · Impact

A learner contributing at their true capability level. An employed worker. Evidence for your programme. Social value for your sector.

Tangible output — Pillar 1: Identity

Every learner receives a
personalised identity statement.

Before a single vocabulary word is taught, EmployaLingua® generates a bilingual identity statement for each learner — in English and their native language. It names their background, their capability, and their professional story.

A learner who has been genuinely seen before being asked to learn is a learner who completes. This is what drives engagement, retention, and employment outcomes — not gamification alone.
