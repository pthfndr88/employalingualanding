The language of work.

Work has its own language. EmployaLingua helps people build employability skills, understand workplace communication and practise the language they need to take their next step.

Two pathways, one purpose

Whether someone is preparing for their first opportunity or developing English for a particular industry, learning should connect to the realities of work.

Employability for work

Explore career readiness, communication, applications, interviews and workplace expectations. Designed for young people and adults preparing for their next opportunity.

Language for work

Develop English and sector-specific vocabulary through practical activities. Bilingual support can help learners connect what they already know with how they communicate at work.

Learning that fits around real life

EmployaLingua combines accessible digital practice with learning activities that can support facilitated programmes.
