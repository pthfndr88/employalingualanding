Stockport context

Sector-specific language is the barrier between Stockport's ESOL learners and the work they can do.

Stockport has significant ESOL learner populations with strong employment capability. The gap is not competence — it is the sector-specific language of Healthcare, Construction, Teaching, and Digital roles.

EmployaLingua® closes that gap. Deployed alongside Stockport College and Stockport Council's existing ESOL provision, it converts language learners into sector-ready workers.
