The distinction

General fluency and vocational fluency are not the same thing. Standard ESOL was never designed to close the underemployment gap.

A learner can hold a conversation in English and still be unable to read a care plan, follow a site induction, or respond to a clinical handover. The barrier is not conversational English — it is the sector-specific language of the role they are entering.

And many of these learners are not unemployed. They are underemployed — a Syrian doctor working as a care assistant, a Colombian engineer labouring on a construction site, an Ethiopian accountant in a hotel kitchen. Technically capable, professionally experienced, working below their level because the language of the sector is the only thing standing between them and the work they can actually do.

EmployaLingua® is built for both. Not as a supplement to ESOL provision — as the mechanism that makes ESOL provision produce sector-ready workers, not just English speakers.

Dignity-infused AI conversationalists Proprietary

EmployaLingua®'s AI tutors are not generic chatbots. They are built on a dignity-first design model — every interaction is framed around what the learner can do, not what they can't. The AI stays in character as a workplace colleague, supervisor, or patient while simultaneously evaluating language proficiency. The learner never feels assessed. They feel heard.

Simultaneous bilingual learning

Most language platforms teach in the target language or translate on demand. EmployaLingua® does both at once. The learner is building English and reinforcing comprehension in their native language in the same activity. For a Syrian Arabic speaker entering a Manchester care home, this means every word they learn in English is anchored in the language they think in — dramatically improving retention and confidence.

Push delivery — trauma-informed by design

Refugees and displaced people carry significant decision fatigue. EmployaLingua® removes one more decision from their day. The platform tells learners exactly what to do next. They tap Start. The path — vocabulary, reading, AI conversation, assessment gate — takes them the rest of the way.

The platform

Sector-specific language.
AI-powered. Evidence-ready.

Designed for cohorts of 20–500 learners. Commission as a standalone vocational fluency strand or alongside PthFndR Trapezium delivery for full pre-to-employment measurement.

Dignity-infused AI conversationalists

Proprietary

AI tutors that stay in character as workplace colleagues — virtual supervisor, patient, family member, or interviewer — while evaluating language proficiency in real time. Dignity-first by design: they build on what the learner can do, never expose what they can't.

Simultaneous bilingual learning

English and the learner's native language — Arabic, Urdu, Farsi, or Tigrinya — taught in the same activity, at the same time. Not translation on demand. Simultaneous acquisition that anchors new vocabulary in the language the learner thinks in.

Sector-specific vocabulary

Not general English. The words, abbreviations, process language, and professional register of Healthcare, Construction, Digital, or Teaching — designed per sector × CEFR level by your admin curriculum team.

CEFR-tracked progression

Language proficiency tracked across Range, Accuracy, Fluency, and Coherence at every level. Assessment gates at A1, A2, B1, and B2 must be passed before the path advances — producing standards-aligned, reportable evidence per learner.

Smart CV + CEFR Passport

Every activity auto-builds a bilingual professional profile. The CEFR Passport describes what the learner can do — in English and their native language — in plain, confident terms an employer can act on. Built automatically. No extra effort from the learner.

Gamified push delivery

Streaks, milestones, and visible progress. Content delivered proactively — learners tap Start, the path does the rest. Designed for people carrying the decision fatigue of resettlement. No menu navigation. No choices about what to study. Just progress.
