Oldham context

Oldham's ESOL population is one of GM's largest — and most underserved by vocational provision.

Oldham consistently features among England's most deprived local authorities. The borough has a large and longstanding South Asian community with strong employment ambition but limited access to sector-specific vocational English.

The Royal Oldham Hospital — part of the Northern Care Alliance — is the primary NHS employer. Oldham College is the primary FE provider. EmployaLingua®'s Healthcare and Teaching pathways address Oldham's most acute workforce gaps.
