How it works

Before we teach you the words,
we find yours.

EmployaLingua® is built on the Trapezium Model: Identity, Character, Competence, Impact. Your path begins not with vocabulary — but with who you are.

1

Receive your identity statement

EmployaLingua® generates a personalised identity statement for you — in English and your native language. It names your background, your experience, and your capability. Your path is built from there, not from scratch.

2

Learn in two languages at once

Your AI tutor speaks your language and English simultaneously. You build sector-specific vocabulary while your home language anchors every new word. Arabic. Urdu. Farsi. Tigrinya.

3

Complete missions. Build your CV.

Every activity you finish adds to your professional profile. Pass a conversation simulation. Master a vocabulary set. Your bilingual Smart CV builds itself automatically — ready for an employer to act on.

Your practice partner

Practice real conversations.
Zero pressure.

Your AI tutor plays the role of a supervisor, a patient, a colleague, or a job interviewer. It speaks your language. It understands your level. It never makes you feel judged — only prepared.

Practice a handover conversation in a care home. Respond to a site supervisor on a construction job. Introduce yourself in a school staffroom. All before your first shift.

This is your dignity-infused AI conversationalist. It builds on what you can do. It never exposes what you can't.

The Trapezium Model

Your journey through
Identity. Character. Competence. Impact.

Every EmployaLingua® learner moves through four stages. The sequence is deliberate. Skills built on sand don't hold. Identity comes first — because without knowing who you are, everything built on top is unstable.

1

Identity

Who are you? Your background, your capability, your professional story — named before learning begins. You receive a personalised identity statement in English and your native language.

KNOW · A1 · A2
2

Character

Your values, your working style, your professional behaviours. Character shapes how you apply what you know in a real workplace — and how you show up on day one.

BE · A2 · B1
3

Competence

The sector-specific language of the role — built on a stable foundation. You are doing the job you are qualified to do. Your CEFR Passport and Smart CV evidence everything you have achieved.

DO · B1 · B2 · C1
4

Impact

You are contributing at your true capability level. Your language and your professional identity have grown together — and so has your impact on the people around you.

DO · B2 · C1

How it was built

Not built in an office.
Built with the people it serves.

EmployaLingua® was built in Manchester with MEaP and BRASS — tested by real learners who were, like you, qualified and capable and working below their level.

People who had qualifications, careers, and experience — in other countries, in other languages. The platform was changed based on what they said. The identity statement. The bilingual bridge. The dignity-infused AI. All of it shaped by the people it was built for.
