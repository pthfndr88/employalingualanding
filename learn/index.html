How it works

Before we teach you the words,
we find yours.

Three steps. No classrooms. No waiting lists. Just open the platform and start.

1

You receive your identity statement

EmployaLingua® opens with a short conversation in your language. It surfaces your background, your qualifications, and what you already know — then generates a personalised identity statement. Your path is built from there, not from scratch.

2

You practise in a real job, every day

From your first session, you are placed inside real workplace scenarios — a ward, a building site, a classroom, an office. Your AI tutor speaks English and your native language at the same time. No judgement. No wrong answers. Just practice.

3

Your CV builds itself as you go

Every mission you complete adds to your professional profile. Your bilingual Smart CV and CEFR Passport update automatically — so by the time you finish, you have real evidence of what you can do. Ready for any employer to act on.

Your practice partner

Real conversations.
Zero pressure.

Your AI tutor plays the role of a supervisor, a patient, a colleague, or a job interviewer. It speaks your language. It meets you at your level. It never makes you feel judged — only prepared.

Practice a handover conversation in a care home. Respond to a site supervisor. Introduce yourself in a school staffroom. All before your first shift.
