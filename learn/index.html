How it works

Three things. That's it.

You don't have to decide what to study, how to study it, or where to start. The app knows.

1

Choose your sector

Healthcare, Construction, Digital, or Teaching. Everything you learn will be the real language of that sector — the words, phrases, and conversations you'll actually need at work.

2

Follow your path

Your path is ready. Vocabulary. Reading passages. Conversations with a virtual colleague, supervisor, or patient. Each activity is set at exactly your level. You just tap Start.

3

Watch your CV build

Every activity you complete adds to your professional profile. By the end, you have a real CV with real evidence — how many words you know, what you've read, which conversations you've passed.

The path

You don't have to figure out what to study today.
The path tells you exactly what to do next.

Vocabulary activities. Workplace reading passages. AI conversation simulations where you practise talking to a virtual supervisor, patient, or interviewer. Each one at your level. Each one building on the last. When you complete a level, you pass an assessment gate — and your CEFR Passport updates automatically.

What you earn

By the time you finish,
you have proof.

Every activity you complete builds two things at once: your language ability, and your professional profile. You don't have to write your CV. It writes itself as you go.

The CEFR Passport describes what you can do in plain, confident language — not a list of what you struggled with. The Smart CV shows an employer exactly what you've achieved: words mastered, passages read, conversations passed.

Both are available in English and your home language. Both are yours to download and share.

How it was built

Built by listening
to people like you.

EmployaLingua® was not designed in an office and handed to learners. It was built in Manchester, tested by real people going through exactly what you are going through — new country, new language, new start — and changed based on what they said.

The words, the activities, the path, the way the app speaks to you — all of it was shaped by the feedback of people who used the prototype first. If something did not work for them, it was fixed before it reached you.
