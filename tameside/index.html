Tameside context

Tameside has some of the highest deprivation indices in England — and some of the most motivated ESOL learners.

Tameside Metropolitan Borough Council's employment and skills agenda is directly aligned with EmployaLingua®'s model. The borough's significant Bangladeshi and Pakistani communities are among the most underemployed in Greater Manchester — not through lack of qualification or ambition, but through the sector-specific language barrier.

Tameside & Glossop Integrated Care is the primary NHS employer. Tameside College is the primary FE provider. EmployaLingua®'s Healthcare and Teaching pathways are built precisely for Tameside's workforce priorities.
