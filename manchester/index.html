Manchester context

Manchester has one of the largest and most diverse ESOL populations in England.

Manchester City Council, the NHS, and the construction sector are among the largest employers of ESOL learners in the region. The gap between ESOL completion and employment remains significant — not because learners lack capability, but because general English provision doesn't close the sector-specific language gap.

EmployaLingua® was prototype-tested in Manchester with MEaP and BRASS — both Manchester-based organisations. The platform was shaped by Manchester learners, for Manchester provision.

Manchester programme context

Making Education a Priority (MEaP)

Befriending Refugees and Asylum Seekers (BRASS)

DWP-funded employability provision
